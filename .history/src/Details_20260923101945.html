flag Mission & Opportunity Overview

At InclusiveTech Labs, we are building software foundations where digital access is engineered from the first commit—never retrofitted as an afterthought. We are seeking a Senior Accessible Frontend Engineer & Design Technologist to lead our global component architecture and champion WCAG 2.2 Level AAA benchmarks across our flagship cloud services.

In this role, you will bridge the creative nuances of design systems with the algorithmic rigor of accessible web components. You will work closely with deaf, blind, low-vision, neurodivergent, and motor-disabled engineers and usability testers to invent frictionless digital interactions that set industry standards.

"We actively encourage candidates who identify as disabled, chronically ill, neurodivergent, or who utilize assistive technologies in their daily lives to apply. Your lived expertise with assistive interfaces is recognized as a direct technical superpower."

terminal What You'll Drive & Deliver

1 Design System Architecture & WCAG AAA Component Engineering

Architect, refactor, and document robust, reusable UI components using TypeScript, React, and semantic HTML5. Ensure all interactive primitives (comboboxes, modal dialogs, complex data grids) strictly respect the WAI-ARIA 1.2 authoring patterns and keyboard navigation paradigms.

2 Automated & Manual Screen Reader Diagnostics

Build continuous integration pipelines combining Axe-core, Playwright A11y tests, and Pa11y checks. Conduct hands-on auditory testing across screen reader combinations (VoiceOver on Safari/iOS, NVDA on Firefox, JAWS on Chrome) to eradicate cognitive noise and focus traps.

3 Inclusive Design Advocacy & Engineering Mentorship

Mentor 14+ frontend engineers across product squads in accessible DOM layout, ARIA live region management, and motion sickness prevention (prefers-reduced-motion). Organize internal hackathons and open-source contributions to universal assistive utilities.
